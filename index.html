Prostadine Ingredients

Prostadine is a liquid dietary supplement marketed to support prostate health and everyday urinary wellness in adult men. Its advertised formula contains nine named ingredients, including seaweed extracts, botanical ingredients, and iodine. Each ingredient has its own nutritional or traditional background, but the benefits of the complete Prostadine formula have not been established by strong clinical evidence.

Inside every serving of Prostadine, you'll find:

1

Nori Yaki Extract Powder

Nori is a red edible seaweed that is often used in Japanese dishes. The nori naturally has different components depending on the source. Prostadine mentions nori as one of the ingredients in its seaweed-based blend.
2

Wakame Extract

Wakame is a brown seaweed used in a variety of Asian dishes. It contains minerals, including iodine, and naturally occurring polysaccharides. Its presence contributes to the marine-derived ingredient profile of Prostadine.
3

Kelp Powder

Kelp is a type of brown seaweed known for its iodine content. Iodine is an essential mineral required for normal thyroid hormone production. The amount of iodine in kelp can vary considerably, depending on the species and processing method.
4

Bladderwrack Powder

Bladderwrack is a brown seaweed traditionally used in herbal preparations. It contains iodine and other naturally occurring compounds. Because its iodine content can vary, the amount consumed is important, particularly for people with thyroid conditions.
5

Saw Palmetto

Saw palmetto is a botanical ingredient derived from the berries of the Serenoa repens palm. It is frequently included in supplements marketed for men's prostate wellness. However, research on its effectiveness for urinary symptoms associated with an enlarged prostate has produced mixed results.
6

Pomegranate Extract

Pomegranate is a fruit containing polyphenols and other antioxidant compounds. Its extract is used in dietary supplements because of its naturally occurring plant constituents. Research into pomegranate and prostate health is ongoing, and evidence about the fruit does not establish the effectiveness of Prostadine itself.
7

Iodine

Iodine is an essential mineral that helps the body produce thyroid hormones involved in metabolism and other normal bodily functions. Prostadine lists iodine separately from its seaweed ingredients. Excessive iodine intake can disrupt thyroid function, so the total amount from all sources matters.
8

Shilajit

Shilajit is a dark, mineral-rich resin traditionally used in Ayurvedic practices. It contains organic compounds, including fulvic acid. Its composition and purity depend on sourcing and processing, making appropriate quality testing important when considering a supplement.
9

Neem

Neem is a plant traditionally used in Ayurvedic preparations. Its leaves and other plant parts contain various naturally occurring compounds. The effects and safety of neem depend on the preparation and amount used, and strong evidence supporting its role in prostate health is limited.
